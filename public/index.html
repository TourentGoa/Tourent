    Goa Transport & RTO Regulations

    Rental Terms & Policies

    Official self-drive rental rules, refundable deposits, safety guidelines & traffic laws in Goa.

    🪪

    Driver Eligibility & Documentation (Cars)

    Documents required at vehicle handover

    • Age Requirement: Minimum driver age is 18 years.
    • Driving Licence: Valid original Indian Driving Licence (LMV - Light Motor Vehicle) or International Driving Permit (IDP) required. DigiLocker/mParivahan verified digital copies are accepted.
    • Identity Proof: Original Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID must be presented for verification.
    🚗

    Car Rental Guidelines & Off-Roading Restrictions

    Permitted usage and boundary limits

    • Goa State Boundary: Vehicles are licensed for operation across North and South Goa. Interstate travel (Maharashtra/Karnataka) requires prior RTO permit approval from Tourent.
    • Strict Beach Driving Prohibition: Driving on beaches, coastlines, sand dunes, or sea water is strictly prohibited by Goa Law (Section 188 IPC) with heavy police penalties and vehicle impoundment.
    • Fuel Policy: Vehicles are delivered with a documented fuel level and must be returned with the same fuel level (Level-to-Level).
    • Unlimited Kilometers: All Tourent self-drive cars include 100% unlimited km across Goa with zero distance surcharges.
    ⏰

    Standard 24-Hour Rental Timing Cycle (09:00 AM – 09:00 AM)

    Mandatory Goa rental duration & return policy

    • Fixed 09:00 AM to 09:00 AM Rental Cycle: In Goa, all vehicle rentals operate on a standard daily cycle from 09:00 AM to 09:00 AM next morning.
    • Afternoon / Evening Pickups (e.g. 06:00 PM): If a customer collects a vehicle in the afternoon or evening (e.g. at 06:00 PM), the vehicle must compulsorily be returned by 09:00 AM the next morning.
    • Late Returns & Extensions: Returning after 09:00 AM will incur an additional day charge unless prior extension approval is obtained from Tourent Admin via WhatsApp.
    🛠️

    Vehicle Damage & Repair Responsibility (Cars)

    Customer liability in case of physical damage or accidents

    • Customer Liability: If any accidental damage, dent, deep scratch, bumper crack, broken part, or body damage is found upon return inspection, the customer is 100% responsible for getting it repaired or making the payment for the damage.
    • Pre-Handover Check: A 360° photo/video check is done at pickup so customers are never billed for pre-existing scratches.
    🛵

    Rider Eligibility & Documentation (Bikes & Scooters)

    Requirements for Activa, Fascino, Hunter 350 & Himalayan

    • Age Requirement: Minimum rider age is 18 years.
    • Driving Licence: Valid Two-Wheeler Driving Licence (MCWG / MCWOG) required. Learner licenses (LL) are not permitted.
    • Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card or Passport copy required at handover.
    • Zero Security Deposit: ₹0 Security Deposit required for all two-wheelers and scooters.
    ⛑️

    Mandatory Helmet & Pillion Rules in Goa

    Strictly enforced across Goa by Traffic Police

    • Double Helmet Law: Helmets are 100% mandatory for BOTH the Rider and Pillion Passenger across all Goa highways, bridges, and city roads (Section 129 Motor Vehicles Act).
    • ISI Helmets Provided: Tourent provides clean, sanitized ISI-certified helmets at vehicle handover.
    • Triple Riding Prohibition: Triple riding on two-wheelers is strictly prohibited and results in immediate automated e-challans.
    ⏰

    Standard Bike/Scooter Rental Timing Cycle (09:00 AM – 09:00 AM)

    Mandatory return timing policy for two-wheelers in Goa

    • 09:00 AM to 09:00 AM Daily Slot: All scooter and motorcycle rentals operate on the standard Goa 09:00 AM to 09:00 AM next morning cycle.
    • Evening Handover (e.g. 06:00 PM): If you pick up a scooter at 06:00 PM in the evening, the vehicle must compulsorily be returned by 09:00 AM the next morning.
    🛠️

    Two-Wheeler Damage Responsibility

    Customer liability in case of fall, collision or damage

    • Customer Liability: If any damage, broken mirror, lever bend, panel crack, or scratch is found upon return inspection, the customer is 100% responsible for repairing it or paying the damage charges.
    🛡️

    🛠️

    Vehicle Damage & Repair Responsibility Policy

    Mandatory policy for all vehicle rentals

    • Damage Settlement: If any accidental damage, body dent, deep scratch, or broken part is found upon vehicle return, the customer is 100% responsible for getting it repaired or paying the full repair/replacement payment before checkout.
    • Pre-existing Verification: Pre-handover inspection photos ensure you are only responsible for new damage incurred during your trip.
    🚦

    Goa Speed Limits & Traffic Safety Regulations

    Avoid automated AI traffic camera challans

    • Speed Limits:
      • National Highways (NH66, NH566): 70–80 km/h
      • City Roads, Beach Towns & Coastal Belts: 40 km/h
      • Signature Bridges (Atal Setu, Zuari Bridge): 40–50 km/h
    • Zero Tolerance for Drink & Drive: Strict breathalyzer checks are conducted nightly across Calangute, Baga, Candolim, and Panaji. Driving under the influence is an offense leading to arrest and vehicle seizure.
    • Seat Belts & Helmets: Mandatory for all passengers (Double helmets for 2-wheelers, seat belts for cars).
    • Traffic Challans: Any AI camera or police e-challan generated during your trip will be settled from the deposit or billed to the customer reference.
